How it works

Three steps.

  1. 01

    You ask

    Pick a mood and click. Your browser sends a request to the server. No account, no sign-up.

  2. 02

    AI writes

    Gemini composes an original quote on the spot. It is never copied or pulled from a list.

  3. 03

    It's kept

    The quote is stored in a Postgres database and joins the public collection above.
